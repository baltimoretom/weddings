# Maison Bridgette — Haute Wedding Architecture & Private Atelier

A high-end, turnkey editorial boutique wedding planning website built for the ultra-elite bride who demands effortless perfection.

## Cloudflare Pages Deployment Guide

This project is a static site (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) and requires zero build steps.

### Deployment Settings in Cloudflare Dashboard:
1. **Log in** to your Cloudflare Dashboard and navigate to **Workers & Pages** > **Create application** > **Pages** > **Connect to Git**.
2. Select the repository: `baltimoretom/weddings`.
3. In **Set up builds and deployments**:
   - **Framework preset:** `None`
   - **Build command:** *(leave empty)*
   - **Build output directory:** `.` or `/` *(root directory)*
4. Click **Save and Deploy**.

Within seconds, Cloudflare Pages will deploy your site globally on Cloudflare's edge network with free automatic SSL.

## Architecture & Features

- **The Zero-Hour Turnkey Retainer Model:** Strategic positioning for high-net-worth clients who require complete in-house execution with zero micromanagement.
- **The Four In-House Pillars:**
  1. *Runway & Haute Couture Access* (Private salons & on-site fittings)
  2. *In-House Botanical Architecture* (Rare stem procurement & sculptural floral installations)
  3. *Unlisted Private Estates* (Historic Maryland valleys, waterfronts, and destination sanctuaries)
  4. *Michelin Gastronomy & Symphony Production*
- **Interactive Delegation Matrix:** Visual comparison of planning time required from the bride (3.5 hours total vs. 400+ hours traditional).
- **Private Dossier Modal & Inquiry Form:** Direct bride and family office / executive assistant intake options with immediate mutual NDA execution.
- **Visuals:** High-resolution editorial photography styled after *Vogue Weddings* and *Architectural Digest*.
